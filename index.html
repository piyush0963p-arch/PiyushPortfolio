FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

Website design, frontend development, WordPress and Wix development, dashboards & web apps, landing pages and UI/UX design.

How do you estimate project pricing or quotes?

Every project has unique requirements, scope, and objectives. Rather than fixed rates, I provide transparent, tailored quotes based on your specific deliverables, timeline, and complexity after an initial discussion. Feel free to reach out with your project details for a custom estimate.

How long does a project take?

A landing page takes 3–5 days, a business website 1–2 weeks, and a larger WordPress/Wix site 2–4 weeks.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes — 2 weeks of free post-launch support for bug fixes and small tweaks. Extended support packages are available.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every site is built mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.
