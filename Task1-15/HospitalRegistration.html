Patient Guidelines

Welcome to CityCare Hospital. Please read the following before registering:

  • Bring valid ID: Please carry a government-issued photo ID to your appointment.

  • Masks required: Face masks are mandatory in all waiting areas.
  • Assistance available: Wheelchairs and staff assistance are available at the main entrance.
