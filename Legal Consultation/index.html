Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about LexAI? Find quick answers below, or get in touch with our team.

Is LexAI a law firm? Can it replace my human attorney?

No, LexAI is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation or legal advice. It is an AI-powered legal software tool designed to review documents, draft template clauses, and streamline law practice. For complex disputes or specialized guidance, we recommend hiring one of our vetted partner attorneys.

How secure is my data on your platform?

Data security is our top priority. We use bank-grade AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Our servers are hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers, and your documents are strictly confidential — we do not use your private documents to train our public models.

How do I consult with a human attorney on LexAI?

Simply log into your dashboard, navigate to the Attorneys Directory, search by practice area or rating, view real-time availability slots, and book a secure video consultation. Our system handles everything from calendar scheduling, secure video chat, and billing.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly through your dashboard settings. If you choose yearly billing, your pricing is locked in at a 20% discount for the full year.
