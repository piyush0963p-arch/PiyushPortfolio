📝 Property Description

Experience unparalleled luxury at Pearl Heights Penthouse — a 4BHK masterpiece perched on the 32nd floor of Hyderabad's most prestigious address. This architectural gem offers panoramic views of the city skyline and the serene Hussain Sagar Lake.


Designed by renowned architect Prashant Mehta, the penthouse features floor-to-ceiling glass walls, an expansive private terrace, a chef's kitchen with imported Italian marble, and a private plunge pool. Each bedroom is an en-suite sanctuary with walk-in wardrobes and smart home automation throughout.


Located in Jubilee Hills — Hyderabad's most coveted neighborhood — with proximity to top schools (Oakridge International, 0.8 km), Apollo Hospital (1.2 km), and Jubilee Hills Road No. 36's premium dining & retail strip.
